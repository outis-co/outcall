How it works

We wire a voice-calling connector into the AI stack you already use. No new app to learn — the phone call is the interface.

STEP 1

We set up your line

A dedicated number and a voice agent, configured with your check-in scripts, your schedule, and your words for things.

STEP 2

Your AI calls you

Chat message went unanswered? The call is the escalation. It rings at the time you chose and asks the questions you defined.

STEP 3

You just answer

Speak naturally — a sentence is a complete answer. The transcript lands back in your AI's hands and your trackers update themselves.

What a call sounds like

Twenty seconds, on your way home. That's the whole interaction.

Your AI: "Evening check-in. How did today land — workout done? And the two numbers you track?"

You: "Workout done. First one zero, second one — yeah, failed today."

Your AI: "Logged. Talk tomorrow."

No app to open. No form to fill. The record is written before you've parked the car.
